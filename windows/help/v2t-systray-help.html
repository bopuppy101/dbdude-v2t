Making the Voice2Text Icon Always Visible

By default, Windows may hide the Voice2Text icon in the overflow area of your system tray. Moving it to the always-visible area lets you monitor V2T's status at a glance.

Why This Matters

The V2T icon changes color to show you what's happening:

Icon Color Status
Blue Idle – ready to record
Green Recording – capturing your voice
Amber Transcribing – processing audio to text

If the icon stays hidden, you can't see these status changes. Moving it to the visible area keeps you informed.

How to Move the Icon

  1. Click the up arrow (^) on your taskbar to open the hidden icons area (system tray overflow).
  2. Find the V2T icon – it looks like this:
  3. Drag the V2T icon from the popup and drop it directly on the down arrow (˅) on the taskbar. (Dropping it to the right of the arrow won't work – it must be dropped on the arrow itself.)

Tip: Windows remembers this setting. You only need to do this once, and the V2T icon will stay visible even after restarts.
